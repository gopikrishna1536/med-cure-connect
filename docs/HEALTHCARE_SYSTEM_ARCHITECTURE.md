# MedCure Connect - Telehealth & EHR Architecture

Comprehensive clinical systems architecture adhering to HIPAA and HL7 FHIR standards.

## Clinical Architecture
1. **Telehealth Video Gateways**: WebRTC encrypted peer-to-peer audio-video consultation tunnels.
2. **Electronic Health Records (EHR)**: FHIR R4 schema compliance for observations, lab diagnostics, and encounters.
3. **Prescription Security**: HMAC cryptographic digital signatures guaranteeing prescription immutability against pharmacy alterations.
